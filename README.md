
# 🍔 BITE RUN – Food Delivery App

### UI/UX Mobile Application Design

## 📌 About the Project

BITE RUN is a modern and user friendly food delivery mobile application designed to make online food ordering simple and convenient.

Users can explore different food items, browse food categories, check prices and ratings, add their favourite meals to the cart, manage quantities and proceed to checkout.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Provide an easy food ordering experience.
- Display food items with images and prices.
- Make navigation simple and user-friendly.
- Allow users to manage their shopping cart.
- Create an attractive and consistent mobile interface.

## 👥 Target Audience

- Students
- Working professionals
- Families
- Online food lovers
- People with busy lifestyles

## 📱 App Screens

### 01 – Welcome Screen
Introduces the BITE RUN application and provides users with a Get Started option.

### 02 – Home Screen
Displays food categories, promotional offers, search bar and popular food items.

### 03 – Food Menu Screen
Allows users to explore pizza options, view ratings and prices, and add food items to their cart.

### 04 – Shopping Cart Screen
Displays selected food items, quantity controls, subtotal, delivery fee and total amount with a checkout option.
